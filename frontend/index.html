A LITTLE PEACE OF MIND

Meet your book’s health check.

Check before you sideload

SpineGuard examines archive integrity, EPUB structure, reading order, resources, and common compatibility concerns. A clean scan means no problems were detected by the checks that ran; it cannot guarantee any reader’s behavior.

A repair, with a paper trail

Repair creates a separate EPUB and checks it again. You’ll see each change and any issues left to resolve. Missing content, damaged compressed data, and protected books may require a fresh download or the publisher’s help.

Stats you can put in context

Words and chapters come from the reading order. Estimated pages use your words-per-page preference; source pages are the page markers supplied by the publisher. Neither predicts the page count on a particular Kobo.

Your library stays on your computer

Books are processed locally. No account, upload, or analytics service is required. Report exports contain book metadata, issue details, and local file paths.
